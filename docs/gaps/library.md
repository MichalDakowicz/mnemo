# Library: design gaps (Main.dc.html vs Radar)

- **Filters panel**: missing in design (the artboard has a "Filters" pill and no panel). Radar has a filter sheet: streaming service (incl. "My services" and Other), genre, director, release year, OR inside a facet, AND across. Stopgap: a glass popover under the pill with chips from the same tokens, a find box, top 24 values per facet, "Clear filters" and "Done".
- **Sort menu**: design has a "Recently added" pill with a chevron, no menu and no direction control. Radar has six sorts plus an asc/desc toggle. Stopgap: glass dropdown with the six sorts and an arrow icon button for direction.
- **Grid sizes**: design shows one grid size. Radar has compact / normal / large. Stopgap: three small chips beside the view toggles (grid view only), mapped to different `minmax` column widths.
- **Status chips**: design has All / Watchlist / Watching / Watched / Dropped. Radar has no "Dropped" and adds "Rewatch" (watched and still on the watchlist). Stopgap: chips follow Radar (All, Watchlist, Watching, Watched, Rewatch). "Dropped" is not built because the data has no such state. A dropped badge is not rendered either.
- **Quick status change from a card**: not in design; Radar's cards only open the edit screen. Stopgap: a small "..." glass button on the cover (hover/focus; always visible on touch) opening a menu of Watchlist / Watching / Mark watched (films only; a series is finished by ticking episodes). "Mark watched" leaves the watchlist (a finish); the Watchlist option on a watched title keeps `watched` (the rewatch case), same as Radar's flag helpers.
- **Random pick**: not in design. Radar has a spinning reel sheet. Stopgap: a "Random" pill picks one title (watchlist pool when no status chip is set, else the filtered list) and shows a small glass card with Open / Another. No spin animation.
- **Add / quick add**: the artboard has no add control (Search in the dock goes to Discover). Radar has a quick-add sheet with status picker, ratings and "Add manually". Stopgap: an "Add" pill that links to `/discover?focus=search`. Add-by-search and manual add (TMDB cannot find it) are not built in Library; manual add needs a home (Discover or a sheet).
- **Manually added titles (no TMDB id)**: Radar opens its edit screen. Mnemo's title route needs a TMDB id, so these cards render without a link.
- **Continue watching card**: design shows backdrop art plus "Next · N. Episode name". Rows hold no backdrop or episode names (those need a TMDB season fetch per card). Stopgap: poster image cropped to 16:9, "Next · season S, episode E" from the watched-episode log. Films in progress show "Watching" and no counter. The check button in the card is decorative on hover, as in the design (marking an episode watched is the detail page's job).
- **Coming soon**: design shows "From your watchlist · next 6 months" and a Film / Season premiere line. Radar's selection has no 6-month cap (any unreleased title, up to 30) and no season-premiere data. Stopgap: header is a collapse toggle ("From your watchlist · N", persisted by `comingSoonCollapsed`), second line is Film or Series.
- **Recently added**: not in design; Radar shows it when Settings "Show recently added" is on (window in days). Stopgap: a section of ordinary cards above "All titles". Titles in any section are removed from the main grid (as Radar does).
- **Footer / sync "?" button**: design has a floating "?" opening a sync/version bar. Not built here (shell-level; needs the app version and auth state).
- **List view**: design has the toggle but no list layout. Stopgap: 44px poster, title, director · year · runtime, rating, status text.
- **Empty / loading / error**: design has one "Nothing here" state. Added honest loading, load-error and empty-library variants in the same style.
